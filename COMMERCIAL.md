# Company-profile-led advertisements

Use a dated, evidence-led company profile before choosing a treatment. Record the product/service, setting, verified differentiator, voice, identity assets, desired customer action and allowed claims. Separate sourced facts, targeting hypotheses and creative proposals. Cite the actual first-party evidence for each commercial claim; a website image does not authorize casting its staff or customers. Keep confidential profiles and production records outside this repository.

## Choose the idea before the generation route

Offer materially distinct treatments, such as a human story, observed craft, humor or a product demonstration. For each, describe the visible product action, causal payoff and business fit. A different crop, bars, reordered clips or another prompt is not by itself a distinct treatment. Record the owner's selection and retain rejected options. Technical success cannot reverse a creative rejection.

Use original, clearly adult fictional performers. Do not copy real people or infer protected traits from customer stereotypes. Approve appearance, wardrobe, behavior and location design; keep identity, environment, props and action geometry as separate reference roles. Record actual reference bytes/hash, rights, approval and supported provider IDs before calling a reference bound. A planned reference is not a generated or approved asset.

## Turn the treatment into visible action

Every shot should make the product/service interaction readable. Record purpose, start/action/end, support/contact geometry, source and intended retained duration, axis/camera side, focal-length look, focus, light, screen direction, hand/prop state, outgoing/incoming state and sound. Do not show only an attractive person beside a product when the chosen idea depends on using it.

Set a source-duration ceiling from the current brief. The owner's current advertisement brief caps each source clip at 20 seconds; record `source_cap_seconds: 20` for that project. This is a per-source limit, not a universal final-runtime rule or generic default. Final duration belongs to the selected treatment. Two compliant six-second sources prove neither a twelve-second usable edit nor valid trims. Keep planned and actual source, retained and export coordinates separate; do not invent usable ranges before media review.

Preserve [planning](PLANNING.md), [continuity](CONTINUITY.md) and [performance](PERFORMANCE.md) gates. Motivate angle changes and inserts through action or attention. Carry axis, eyelines, anatomy, product/fluid state and timing across cuts. Identity/state references do not automatically imply a fixed first frame.

## Prepare and execute only the authorized request

Freeze the exact prompt, provider/model/mode/settings and ordered media bindings. Check the current connector contract and realistic geometry before quoting. Do not substitute a model or unsupported reference syntax. Review the prompt separately from its generated result.

A quote is evidence of price, not budget approval or a validated final request. Bind the quote to the exact payload/count/audio/resolution and currency. Distinguish credits from cash and an estimate from an enforced maximum. Include reference creation, attempts, enhancements and retry scope in the authorization plan. If the budget is zero, no new paid operation is allowed. If a hard cap was requested, require verified enforcement or a new explicit decision accepting estimate risk. Do not infer either from a price quote.

Within an already authorized quantity and amount, start with the highest-risk essential interaction when appropriate. Preserve the exact payload/hash, reference hashes and IDs, job ID, quote, actual charge when known and original result. Reconcile uncertain submissions before retrying. Review contact, identity and action before dependent coverage. No automatic retries, subscriptions, installs or access changes are authorized by this workflow.

## Hear and watch the actual result

Keep dialogue, effects/ambience and music stems separable. Label native generated sound as synthetic; do not represent it as a real venue recording. Silent dialogue is allowed, but still needs a considered sound plan. Every speaking voice needs a heard approved source and exact speaker/dialogue ledger.

Independently watch the exact export at normal speed and directly listen to its complete audio. Record artifact hash/version, reviewer, coverage and results for product interaction/contact, identity, comprehension, pacing, seams, sound, attribution, CTA, rights and technical format. Frame inspection supplements full-speed motion review; waveforms and transcripts do not substitute for listening. Missing coverage remains NOT VERIFIED. The owner's verdict is separate, and a numerical rubric does not certify artistic excellence.

Retain original and rejected candidates, reference versions, prompts, stems, editable projects and receipts. Record observed failure separately from its hypothesized cause, then propose one deliberate change and comparison. Publishing requires approval of the exact asset, destination and copy. No local machine resource thresholds are implied by these production rules.

## Optional offline declaration checks

Run `python3 validate_commercial.py examples/fictional-commercial.json` for a synthetic example. The [schema](schemas/commercial.schema.json) and validator check declared durations, reference completeness, exact quote/payload association, budget declaration, explicit rejection and full-picture/direct-audio review records. `status` may be `planning`, `submission_ready`, `accepted` or `rejected`. A reviewer's passing flags cannot override an owner rejection.

These checks do not fetch profiles, decide if treatments are meaningfully different, authenticate permissions/approvals, verify bytes against remote assets, enforce billing, inspect the actual payload, perceive motion or hear sound. `submission_ready` and `accepted` are claims being checked, never authority to execute. Existing planning, performance, rights and release checks still apply. Tests use invented companies and adults, not private production facts.

Source basis: the owner-authorized sanitized commercial integration handoff, reconciled with existing execution, planning, performance and release guidance. Editing/sound principles and exact read coverage remain in [the focused research index](RESEARCH_SOURCES.md). No fresh full-book reading or source-film reuse is claimed.

Before output exists, omit `output`, `review`, `owner_heard_and_watched`, `owner_review_evidence` and `owner_review_artifact`; never fabricate output hashes or listening evidence to pass a planning/submission check. Acceptance requires all five fields with actual declared artifact and review records. The synthetic accepted fixture illustrates field shape, not evidence to copy into a real project.

## Phone-first social delivery

For the owner's current social advertisements, set `social.phone_first: true` and compose natively for 9:16 from reference design through capture/generation. Do not plan a landscape shot and assume a later vertical crop will preserve the action. Phone-first means designed for a phone screen; it does not require selfies, handheld camerawork or a phone-camera aesthetic.

Frame faces, hands, product and their interaction so they remain legible at normal phone viewing size. Put an immediate visible hook in the opening and use short cuts motivated by action, attention or a changed idea. Do not impose a universal cut length, accelerate unreadable actions or lose contact/continuity to increase cut count. Preserve the configured source cap (20 seconds for this brief) and the selected project's final runtime.

Plan for muted comprehension: the visible action, product attribution and intended customer action should make sense without sound. Use readable captions where needed without inventing or changing dialogue. Test the muted export separately from the full direct-audio review; muted readability cannot replace cleared, intelligible sound or approved voices.

Before final export, identify the exact platform, placement and current UI/overlay guide. Record the guide source and check date; verify caption, brand, product and CTA placement against that destination's overlays and crop behavior. There is no universal pixel-safe rectangle. If the destination is unresolved, leave final safe-region verification unresolved rather than inventing values. Verify faces/hands/product remain readable on the actual phone-sized export, including caption contrast and time to read.

Review the clean final social export for its intended orientation, current destination format, first/last frames, unwanted bars/borders/watermarks, legible branding/captions, cleared sound, seams and completion of the visible action. Preserve full normal-speed picture review, direct listening, rights and exact-owner-acceptance gates. Source/tool metadata cannot prove native composition or mobile quality; the optional social checks validate only recorded declarations. Actual social review fields and destination-guide evidence may be omitted before the output exists but are required for phone-first acceptance.

## Required focused research before advertisement production

Use [the research worksheet](templates/commercial-research.md) for every new advertisement. Before finalizing the treatment/payload, inspect relevant social campaign evidence and available academic research through the owner's lawful KU/Library access or public sources. Research should answer the current creative decision, not become an open-ended prerequisite to all progress. Inventory existing notes first and preserve the owner's current selected/rejected treatments; research does not reopen an explicit rejection or authorize changing the concept.

Select campaigns relevant to the product action, audience/use situation, placement and objective. Record exact source title, credited author/publisher, stable link, access route and date, and precisely which pages/sections were read. Track queued, read and unavailable separately. Record whether the actual creative was viewed and directly heard, its version and ranges, or explicitly text-only/not viewed/not heard. An article about an ad is not a viewing of that ad; a thumbnail, waveform or transcript is not full media review.

Distinguish measured business outcomes from popularity. Record the metric's definition, time window, population/denominator, paid distribution when known, attribution/comparator and source. Views, likes, awards or shares alone do not establish sales, conversion or causal lift. Treat an advertiser/platform case study as a sourced claim with possible selection bias, not independently audited proof. If comparable outcome data is unavailable, say so; do not call a popular campaign commercially successful on that basis alone.

For studies/books, note pages/sections actually read, research method/sample, context/date, measured outcome, uncertainty, causal versus correlational design and transfer limits to the current placement/product. Do not claim full-book reading from a preview or search snippet. Distinguish a research-supported principle from the original creative hypothesis that applies it to this ad.

Check available KU/Library access without bypassing access controls. If content cannot be lawfully accessed, record the specific limitation and use a legal public alternative, such as an author/publisher excerpt, open scholarly article or first-party campaign case study. Mark the alternative's narrower evidential scope. Missing access does not justify invented KU reading, unauthorized downloads or an automatic broad research delay. A queued source cannot count as reviewed. Keep KU licensed content within its allowed educational/noncommercial scope; do not import licensed full text into the production repository or treat reading access as a commercial reuse license.

Convert the review into six concrete decisions: hook, visible physical/product action, cut motivation, pacing, sound and payoff. For each, cite the actual reviewed source IDs, label principle versus creative hypothesis, specify a proposed change or justified retention of the approved treatment, and state how the actual output will be evaluated. Do not imitate third-party footage, music, likenesses, scripts or distinctive creative execution. Produce original adult actors and original execution; source study is not asset permission. Validate rights separately.

The research declaration gate requires a reviewed campaign and academic source, or a documented access limitation with an actually reviewed lawful public alternative. It records coverage and application, not scientific validity, completeness, legal entitlement, campaign success or artistic quality. A researcher must still assess relevance and evidence strength. Phone-first native 9:16, current source-duration ceiling, exact budget approval, full picture/direct-audio QA and owner acceptance remain required after research.
